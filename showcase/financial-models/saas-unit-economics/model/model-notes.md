**Decision:** Does a steady growth plan create efficient ARR without using too much cash?

One steady twelve-month customer plan. ARR, CAC, LTV/CAC, payback, and burn. Monthly MRR, ARR, EBITDA, and cash roll-forward plus an annual bridge.

- Horizon: 12 months
