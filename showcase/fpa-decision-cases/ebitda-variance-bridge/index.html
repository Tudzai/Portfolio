CFO read

Three implications for the forecast call.

  1. 01
    Revenue quality comes first.

    Volume and price require separate recovery assumptions; one blended revenue line would hide the commercial risk.

  2. 02
    Cost standards are stale.

    Carrier and fuel pressure should be reflected in job-cost assumptions and surcharge governance now.

  3. 03
    Timing is not a save.

    The $1.6M benefit reverses later, so it cannot support the full-year outlook without a real action plan.

Control

Bridge ties: $37.6M - $9.6M + $1.6M = $29.6M.

Make three calls before the next forecast lock.
