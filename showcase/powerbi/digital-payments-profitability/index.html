What this demonstrates

01

Decision support

Which merchant segments and fee changes can improve unit profit without sacrificing durable payment volume?

02

Finance logic

Payment volume times take rate equals gross payment revenue. Revenue less processing, incentive, and risk cost equals gross profit. Unit profit divides gross profit by completed transactions.

03

Governance

Settled and reversed transactions reconcile to processor totals. Merchant contracts map to one effective fee schedule. Scenario changes preserve volume and cost-unit consistency. All displayed values are synthetic and public-safe.

Portfolio demonstration only. Values are synthetic; no raw source files, PBIX/PBIT files, local paths, or company-confidential data are published.
