What this demonstrates

01

Decision support

Which categories and channels are growing profitable demand, and where are refunds or mix eroding margin?

02

Finance logic

Order value less discounts and refunds equals recognized net sales. Net sales less product and fulfillment cost equals contribution margin. Orders times average order value reconciles to gross merchandise value.

03

Governance

Orders and refunds are deduplicated at transaction level. Category and channel mappings are complete before close. Refund timing is aligned to the original sale cohort. All displayed values are synthetic and public-safe.

Portfolio demonstration only. Values are synthetic; no raw source files, PBIX/PBIT files, local paths, or company-confidential data are published.
