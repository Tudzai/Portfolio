What this demonstrates

01

Decision support

Where is performance off plan, what is driving the EBITDA gap, and which owners must act before forecast sign-off?

02

Finance logic

Revenue minus direct cost minus operating expense equals EBITDA. Actual, budget, and forecast use the same fiscal calendar and FX basis. The variance bridge must sum exactly to the reported EBITDA gap.

03

Governance

P&L tie-out by business unit and region. Forecast version and close date are displayed together. Every material variance has an owner and action status. All displayed values are synthetic and public-safe.

Portfolio demonstration only. Values are synthetic; no raw source files, PBIX/PBIT files, local paths, or company-confidential data are published.
