What this demonstrates

01

Decision support

What is driving regional performance after FX and eliminations, and which close issues threaten the board story?

02

Finance logic

Entity P&Ls translate at the approved period exchange-rate convention. Intercompany eliminations remove matched revenue, cost, balance, and profit. Consolidated results equal translated entities plus approved eliminations.

03

Governance

Every entity passes a local-to-group P&L tie-out. FX rates and elimination journals are version controlled. Close exceptions include owner, materiality, and resolution status. All displayed values are synthetic and public-safe.

Portfolio demonstration only. Values are synthetic; no raw source files, PBIX/PBIT files, local paths, or company-confidential data are published.
