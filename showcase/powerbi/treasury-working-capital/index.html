What this demonstrates

01

Decision support

Is liquidity protected, which working-capital exposures need escalation, and how does downside change runway?

02

Finance logic

Opening cash plus inflows minus operating and financing outflows equals closing cash. DSO uses average receivables divided by credit sales times period days. Runway divides available liquidity by modeled monthly net burn.

03

Governance

Cash balances reconcile to synthetic bank and ledger totals. Receivables aging uses one as-of date and due-date convention. FX exposure and hedges share a common currency and maturity basis. All displayed values are synthetic and public-safe.

Portfolio demonstration only. Values are synthetic; no raw source files, PBIX/PBIT files, local paths, or company-confidential data are published.
